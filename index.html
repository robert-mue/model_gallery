Simile model gallery

Example models, selected for exploring Simile. Each page says what the model is about; its Open in Simile link opens the model in the app, in a new tab, with any files it needs.

  • Bank account

    Money earning interest, with a fixed amount taken out each year. The first model most people build.

  • LAMOS

    A landscape of vegetation, soil water and fire, on a grid of patches that act on their neighbours.

  • Lotka-Volterra predator-prey

    The classic predator-prey model, straight from its two differential equations.

  • Trees

    A population of trees that grow, are recruited and die, each at its own place.

  • Trees over pasture

    Individual trees growing over a grid of pasture patches, shading the pasture beneath them. Opens in three dimensions.

  • Coral acclimation

    Coral reefs, their algae and bleaching in a warming ocean, 1955 to 2100. Published in PLoS Computational Biology, 2022.

  • Pangolin harvest

    How hunting a rare species alongside a common one, at a price that rises with rarity, can drive it extinct. Published in the Journal of Applied Ecology, 2023.

Wildlife population models

From Norman Owen-Smith's book Introduction to Modeling in Wildlife and Resource Conservation (Blackwell, 2007), converted from its TrueBASIC programs and published here with his permission.

  • Elementary population model

    Births and deaths, each a fixed fraction of the population, with a ceiling.

  • Descriptive population models

    Thirteen forms of density dependence, each a model of its own: Exponential growth with a ceiling, Logistic, Ricker, Beverton-Holt, Generalised Beverton-Holt, Theta-logistic, Threshold logistic, Lagged logistic, Logistic with cyclic K, Logistic with random K, Theta-logistic with random K, Generalised Beverton-Holt with random K, Allee effect.

  • An age-structured population, three ways

    The same five age classes built three ways in Simile: A stock per age class, One submodel, five instances, Submodel and "next class" association.

  • Herbivore-vegetation dynamics (Caughley)

    Caughley's interactive herbivore-vegetation system: grazers and their food, oscillating to equilibrium.

  • Random walk of a small population

    Demographic chance in a small population: it wanders at random until it dies out.

  • Habitat choice through the dormant season

    Herbivores choosing among three habitats through the dormant season: matching or maximizing.

A model opened from here is a copy in your own browser: change it as you like, nothing here changes.
