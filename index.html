Simile model gallery

Example models, selected for exploring Simile. Each page says what the model is about; its Open in Simile link opens the model in the app, in a new tab, with any files it needs.

  • Bank account

    Money earning interest, with a fixed amount taken out each year. The first model most people build.

  • Lotka-Volterra predator-prey

    The classic predator-prey model, straight from its two differential equations.

  • Trees

    A population of trees that grow, are recruited and die, each at its own place.

A model opened from here is a copy in your own browser: change it as you like, nothing here changes.
