Bank account

Money in an account earning interest each year, with a fixed amount taken out: one stock, an inflow, an outflow and an interest rate. The first model most people build in Simile.

Run it with a time step of 1 for 10 years: $300 at 10% interest, less $10 a year, grows to $618.75. Change the interest rate and run it again to see the difference.
