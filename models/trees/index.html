Trees

A population of trees, each growing in size, with new trees recruited and old ones dying, and each placed at its own position. Shows a submodel whose instances come and go, a total over the population, and a display of the trees in space.

Run it for 300 years. Plot the trees’ sizes (a line per tree) or the total, or show them in space with the lollipop display.
